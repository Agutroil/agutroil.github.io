02 / World & exploration

A world built from regions,
one trail at a time.

Nythara pairs billboarded, eight-direction 2D characters with a stylized 3D landscape. Trails, landmarks, portals and dungeons form one connected world, and every region is authored by hand.

Leave Dawnwatch, the current city hub, and follow the road into Silverwood. A single readable trail crosses three separated monster territories before a warp portal opens the way to Moonroot Sanctum. Each region has its own light, vegetation and landmark language.

The world already connects forest, highlands, mire, coast, frost and ash realms, two authored dungeons and the Dawnwatch interiors. Progression is content-led: clearing the Hollow Ruins opens the road to Silverwood.

04 / Combat & progression

Fight, grow,
carry it with you.

Combat, quests, gear and travel share one persistent character. The same build and equipment carry from the open world into a dungeon — and into shared encounters with other players.

⚔

Combat

Telegraphed melee and heavy attacks, ranged projectiles, Poison and Slow, boss phases and shared online encounters.

✦

Character progression

Levels 1–20, separate attribute and skill points, three ranks per skill and a four-slot loadout with a free reset.

◈

Inventory & equipment

Rarity, stat comparison, sell values, weapon, body and accessory slots. Equipment changes are confirmed by the server, so your build stays consistent in the shared world.

✧

Quests

Branching dialogue, a journal and tracker, and a progression chain from the Hollow Ruins into Silverwood and Moonroot Sanctum.

◎

World travel

Portals gated by level, quests, items and keys, plus region discovery, a world map and a minimap.

◉

Economy

Crowns, a vendor with buy and sell offers, and loot that feeds crafting, equipment and the next expedition.

⚒

Crafting

Gathering nodes, recipes, a workbench and crafted gear such as the Grove Blade and Mosswoven Vest.

☠

Boss encounters

Authored, phased bosses with telegraphs, first-clear rewards and repeat clears — built for groups as well as solo play.

07 / Online multiplayer alpha

A shared world,
run by the server.

Nythara's online multiplayer alpha connects independent players in one shared, server-authoritative world, with remote play, persistent characters and reconnect support.

Characters, movement, combat, enemies, loot, progression and world state are controlled by the server and synchronised between connected players.

Meet other adventurers in Silverwood, fight the same creatures, talk in local chat and trade items. Characters return after a reconnect or a server restart.

Secure Online Multiplayer Encrypted remote connections
Server authority Shared world state
Persistent characters Character & progress restore
Reconnect Controlled session recovery

Online multiplayer alpha: Remote multiplayer is already implemented and verified with multiple independent clients. Public game-server infrastructure is still in development.

11 / Broad hardware support

Built to run
on modest hardware.

Nythara targets Godot's GL Compatibility renderer and keeps its world presentation inside a low-end budget.

GL Compatibility

Renderer path chosen for integrated graphics and older GPUs.

Scalable profiles

Low, Balanced and High scale grass, vegetation, shadows and clouds.

Batched world

MultiMesh vegetation, shared materials and cached static geometry.

Bounded effects

Pooled damage numbers, no dynamic point lights in world presentation.

No GPU benchmark is claimed here. The low-end profiles are part of the current build, not a promise about every machine.
