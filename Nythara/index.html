01 / The world

A world built from regions,
one trail at a time.

Nythara pairs billboarded, eight-direction 2D characters with a stylized 3D landscape. Trails, landmarks, portals and dungeons are authored into one connected world graph — the server decides where you may go.

Walk from the Dawnwatch road into Silverwood, follow a single readable trail through three separated monster territories, and step through a warp portal into Moonroot Sanctum. Each region has its own light, vegetation and landmark language.

The graph already links forest, highlands, mire, coast, frost and ash realms, eight authored dungeons and the Dawnwatch interiors. Progression is content-led: clearing the Hollow Ruins opens the road to Silverwood.

03 / Gameplay & progression

Every system,
one character.

Combat, quests, gear and travel share one server-authoritative character. The same build and equipment carry from the overworld into a dungeon.

⚔

Combat

Deterministic damage, telegraphed melee and heavy attacks, ranged projectiles, Poison and Slow, and boss phases.

✦

Character progression

Levels 1–20, separate attribute and skill points, three ranks per skill and a four-slot loadout with a free reset.

◈

Inventory & equipment

Rarity, stat comparison, sell values, weapon, body and accessory slots, and server-authoritative equipping.

✧

Quests

Data-driven dialogue, a journal and tracker, and a progression chain from the Hollow Ruins into Silverwood and Moonroot Sanctum.

◎

World travel

A portal network with level, quest, item and lock rules, plus discovery, a world map and a minimap.

◉

Economy

An integer Crown wallet, a vendor with buy and sell offers, and loot that feeds crafting and equipment.

⚒

Crafting

Gathering nodes, recipes, a workbench and crafted gear such as the Grove Blade and Mosswoven Vest.

☠

Boss encounters

Authored, phased bosses with telegraphs, first-clear rewards and repeat clears for normal loot.

06 / Online multiplayer

The server is in charge.

A headless dedicated server owns the simulation. Clients send intents, predict locally and reconcile against authoritative state.

Position, combat, damage, enemy AI, loot, XP, quests, first clears, equipment and respawn all live on the server. That is what makes a character survive a reconnect — and a server restart.

ENet Transport
Authoritative Simulation
Persistent Characters
Co-op Combat & loot

Current scope: development/LAN multiplayer foundation. There is no public production server, no account system and ENet is not encrypted. Do not expose the development server to the internet.

    10 / Broad hardware support

    Built to run
    on modest hardware.

    Nythara targets Godot's GL Compatibility renderer and keeps its world presentation inside a low-end budget.

    GL Compatibility

    Renderer path chosen for integrated graphics and older GPUs.

    Scalable profiles

    Low, Balanced and High scale grass, vegetation, shadows and clouds.

    Batched world

    MultiMesh vegetation, shared materials and cached static geometry.

    Bounded effects

    Pooled damage numbers, no dynamic point lights in world presentation.

    No GPU benchmark is claimed here. Performance evidence and measurements live in the repository's sprint reports and performance notes.
